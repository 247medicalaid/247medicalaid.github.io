    Hour rounding

    When hours are calculated (Timesheet, approvals, exports and My clock), each punch is rounded to the nearest step. The real punch times are kept exactly as they were.

    Location

    Each clock in, lunch and clock out saves where it was made (from the browser: GPS on phones, Wi‑Fi on computers). When it's required, the Time Clock won't record a punch until the agent shares their location.

    Lists

    The options users can be given in Users. Rename an option by editing it, remove it with ×, or add new ones. Removing an option doesn't change the users who already have it. Accounts come from the Monitor.

    Users

    Agents sign in to the Time Clock with their email: add them here and they get an email to create their password. They only see their own Time Clock. Administrative staff are created in the portal.
