    Timesheet

    Pay run

    Hours pay comes from the Timesheet (approved hours on approved days, plus paid time off) up to the day before Assume worked from; the days from then to the end of the pay period are paid in advance at each person's hours per day. Next pay run suggests an adjustment if those days weren't worked. Bonus is in USD; for pesos write COP after the amount (for example -100000 COP). Click a name to change where they're paid from, their aportes, a fixed pay in pesos or their dollar rate.

    Saved pay runs

    Time off

    Approved time off for each person in the year (including what came from Connecteam). A full day counts as 1; time off by the hour counts its hours divided by the person's hours per day. Weekends inside a multi-day time off aren't counted. Click a person to see each date. Tick the boxes to export the day-by-day detail of those people.

    Settings

    Hour rounding

    When hours are calculated (Timesheet, approvals, exports and My clock), each punch is rounded to the nearest step. The real punch times are kept exactly as they were.

    Location

    Each clock in, lunch and clock out saves where it was made (from the browser: GPS on phones, Wi‑Fi on computers). When it's required, the Time Clock won't record a punch until the agent shares their location.

    Lists

    The options users can be given in Users. Rename an option by editing it, remove it with ×, or add new ones. Removing an option doesn't change the users who already have it. Accounts come from the Monitor.

    Pay run

    The dollar is paid at the TRM of the payment day plus an amount, up to a maximum. These are the default values for each new pay run; you can still change them in a pay run. Rete ICA is withheld only from people paid from Nómina 247.

    Import from Connecteam

    Bring your hours history from Connecteam so it isn't lost. In Connecteam, export the Timesheet overview as Excel for the dates you want and upload that file here. You'll see a preview before anything is saved. Days that already have punches in the Time Clock are skipped, so importing the same dates twice doesn't duplicate anything.

    Testing tools

    While the Time Clock is being tested you can erase everything and start over. The portal, Payroll and the Monitor aren't touched. Erasing can't be undone. When testing is over, turn these tools off so nobody can erase the real data.

    Users

    Agents sign in to the Time Clock with their email: add them here and they get an email to create their password. They only see their own Time Clock. Administrative staff are created in the portal.
